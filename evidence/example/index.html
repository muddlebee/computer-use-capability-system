Runtime conditions

The executor distinguishes expected business outcomes, bounded recovery, hard failure, and conditions that require a human.

  • Business outcome Member not found Returns a typed domain result rather than crashing. ResultScreenshot
  • Recovery Session warning Applies one declared recovery and continues replay. ResultEvents
  • Hard failure Permission denied Stops immediately with step, expectation, and observation. ResultScreenshot
  • Human handoff Supervisor approval Pauses automation, transfers the same session, then resumes. RequestResult
